## 1. Large Scale Solar (LSS) rounds
ST selects on competitive (levelised) bid price plus RFP compliance. Each winner is paid its own
bid, so treat LSS as **pay-as-bid**. That is an inference from published results; confirm the award
clause in each RFP.

**Implications for the cost curve:**
- Oversubscription fell from about 13× (LSS3) to about 2× (LSS5+), because lots got larger and
  qualification stricter.
- Since LSS5, tariffs are not disclosed, so calibrate bottom-up.
- Pre-LSS6 tariffs are solar-only and not comparable with hybrid bids.

## 3. CRESS (Corporate Renewable Energy Supply Scheme)
| Item | Finding | Src |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | 26 Jul 2024. Guidelines GP/ST/No.40/2024: 2nd edition 1 Mar 2025, 3rd edition 29 Dec 2025 | [S]/[P] |
| Eligibility | Plant ≥30 MW with direct connection. Green Consumers are high- and medium-voltage consumers in Peninsular Malaysia. Developer ≥51% local ownership | [P] |
| System Access Charge (SAC) at launch | 25 sen/kWh firm, 45 sen/kWh non-firm | [P] |
| SAC cut for RP4 | **Firm 20, non-firm 40** sen/kWh (CREAM charge 15 → 9). Announced in PETRA's 29 Aug 2025 release; tied to the RP4 tariff schedule effective **1 Jul 2025** | [P] |
| Guideline rules | SAC fixed per 3-year regulatory period, changes capped at ±15%. Missed firm output: BESS ≥50% of capacity for 4 h, or the higher SAC applies | [P] |
| **CRESS Acceleration Package, 18 Sep 2026** | Announced by PETRA: firm SAC **14 sen/kWh fixed for 10 years** for projects reaching COD by 31 Dec 2028 (no extensions) with a contract of ≥10 years. Non-firm stays at 40. Guideline amendments expected Nov 2026 | [S] |
| **EC clarification, 13 Oct 2026** | The developer pays 14 sen/kWh. Green consumers pay a **separate 4 sen/kWh**, so the **combined charge is 18 sen/kWh**. The Edge called it an "about-turn" from the 14 sen first understood | [S] (The Star, The Edge, 14–15 Oct 2026) |

Sources: st.gov.my/sites/default/files/2026-04/Guidelines-for-CRESS_2.pdf; ST release on the SAC/CREAM reduction.
**Until the amended guideline is published, treat the package as a Level 2 uncertainty**
(alternative futures, see `portfolio-of-initiatives.md`), not as a fact to price a PPA on. Compare
like with like: firm supply at 14 + 4 = 18 sen/kWh combined against non-firm at 40, a gap of about
22 sen, not 26. Who bears the 4 sen is a CPPA pricing point.

## 4. SELCO and Solar ATAP
**SELCO:** Guideline GP/ST/No.51/2024 (Pin.1/2026), revision registered 15 Jun 2026, dated 21 Jul 2026 [P].
- Thresholds are in **AC** terms.
- Non-domestic systems **>1 MWac** need BESS of ≥1 hour (X MWac → ≥X MWh). Not required until
  after 30 Jun 2026 for systems completed and licence-applied by then [P].
- Charges apply to non-domestic installations of **1 MWac and above** built after 31 Dec 2024 [P].
  The rate of RM12/kWp (cut from RM14 in Feb 2025) is [S]; the charging period is [U].
- Connection assessment study above **72 kWac**; self-assessment at or below 72 kWac [P].
- Source: st.gov.my/sites/default/files/2026-07/Guidelines-for-Solar-Photovoltaic-for-Self-Consumption-In-Peninsular-Malaysia.pdf

**Solar ATAP:** Guideline GP/ST/No.60/2025, in force **1 Jan 2026**, replacing NEM for new applicants [P].
- Export arrangement lasts up to 10 years, then self-consumption only.
- Export credit: domestic users at the energy charge; non-domestic users at the monthly average System
  Marginal Price. Credits offset only the same billing period and are then forfeited.
- Caps: domestic 5 kW single-phase / 15 kW three-phase; non-domestic ≤100% of maximum demand and
  ≤1,000 kW.
- 6.61 MW installed by 30 Apr 2026 [S].

## 5. Demand and policy context
- **NETR (2023):** 70% RE share of installed capacity by 2050 [P]. The interim 31% by 2025
  (12.9 GW) and 40% by 2035 (18 GW) come from the 2021 Malaysia Renewable Energy Roadmap
  (MyRER) [S]. The Deputy PM reported 31% reached by Dec 2025 [S].
- **Installed solar:**
  - NEM 2,903 MW + FiT 345 MW + ATAP 6.61 MW (Dewan Rakyat reply, 30 Apr 2026) [S].
  - LSS 2,237 MW [P].
  - About 5.8 GW in total at end-2025 [S].
- **Data centres** (TNB 2QFY2026 analyst briefing, 28 Aug 2026; position at Jun 2026) [P]:
  - 61 projects, **8.35 GW** secured.
  - 42 projects / 5.65 GW completed; 16 / 2.21 GW under construction; 3 / 0.49 GW with signed
    supply agreements.
  - Actual load 1,256 MW, 67% of it in Johor.
  - This is the demand behind the LSS6 southern steer and CRESS offtake.
- **Cross-border trade:** ENEGEM (Energy Exchange Malaysia), run by the Single Buyer [P].
  - Pilot from 2024; first sale TNB → Sembcorp, 50 MW from Dec 2024 [S].
  - About 375 GWh exported by Oct 2025 [S].
- **Carbon:** Bursa Carbon Exchange launched 9 Dec 2022; first auction 16 Mar 2023 [S].

## 6. Procurement law and consultancy tenders
| Item | Finding | Src |
|---|---|---|
| Statute | **Government Procurement Act 2026 [Act 882]**: Royal Assent 16 May 2026, gazetted 26 May 2026. Commencement by ministerial appointment; **not yet in force** (government expects 2027). Until then the Treasury circulars (PK) govern | [P] (status [S]) |
| Consultancy evaluation (PK 3.3, 29 Nov 2022) | Two stages: **technical 80% / cost 20%**. Technical sub-weights: physical projects firm 35 / staff 30 / methodology 30 / QA 5; **studies firm 25 / staff 35 / methodology 35 / QA 5** | [P] |
| Two-envelope (PK 3.6) | Technical and cost proposals in separate sealed envelopes | [P] |
| Thresholds | Direct appointment with cost ceiling (≥3 shortlisted firms): studies and surveys ≤RM500k; physical projects ≤RM50m project cost. Above these, open tender | [P] |
| Integrity Pact (PK 1.6, in force 1 Jun 2025) | Mandatory. Bidder's declaration goes with the bid; the winner's goes with the letter of acceptance. Beneficial-owner and forced-labour declarations; anti-corruption and anti-bid-rigging clause in every contract | [P] |
| Bumiputera | PK 3.3 shows Bumiputera equity as a *sample, non-mandatory* sub-criterion (10% of the firm score). LSS6 Pakej 2 and 3 are Bumiputera-only | [P] |

Sources: ppp.treasury.gov.my (PK 1.6, 3.1, 3.3, 3.5, 3.6); lom.agc.gov.my (Act 882).

**Implications for a federal "study" tender:**
- Staff (35%) and methodology (35%) carry most of the technical score, so named people and a
  site-specific method win it.
- At 80:20, a 15% fee premium costs about 2.6 points of total score (`tender-playbook.md`).
- State agencies, GLCs and statutory bodies may use their own procurement rules; read each RFP.

## 7. Competition and anti-corruption law
**Competition Act 2010 [Act 712]** [P]:
- s.4(2)(d) deems bid rigging to have the object of significantly preventing, restricting or
  distorting competition (an object infringement).
- Penalty up to **10% of worldwide turnover** over the infringement period (s.40(4)).
- MyCC published bid-rigging handbooks in 2014.
- The Competition (Amendment) Bill 2026 passed both Houses in Jul 2026 and was awaiting Royal Assent
  in Aug 2026 [S]. It adds settlements with up to a 40% penalty reduction. **No merger control yet**
  (deferred) [S].

**MACC Act 2009 [Act 694] s.17A** (in force **1 Jun 2020**, Act A1567) [P]:
- A commercial organisation commits an offence if an associated person gives gratification to obtain
  or retain business or an advantage.
- Penalty: a fine of not less than 10× the gratification or RM1 million, whichever is higher, or up
  to 20 years' imprisonment, or both.
- Directors and management are deemed liable (s.17A(3)).
- Defence: "adequate procedures" (s.17A(4)), per the Guidelines on Adequate Procedures (T.R.U.S.T.
  principles).

## 8. Registrations (for EcoEnergy as a bidder)
- **MOF consultant registration** (PK 3.1) [P]:
  - Codes under 330000 (physical) or 340000 (non-physical). A consultant may work only within its
    registered codes.
  - Relevant codes: 330103 electrical, 330104 mechanical, 330207 environmental studies, 341001
    energy management studies, 341002 green technology studies, 340401 financial studies. Check the
    RFP's required codes against the certificate.
- **Board of Engineers Malaysia (BEM):** under the Registration of Engineers Act 1967 as amended by
  Act A1479 (in force 31 Jul 2015), professional engineering services may be provided only by
  registered persons and registered Engineering Consultancy Practices (s.7A) [S]. The **1998 Scale
  of Fees** still applies; a revision was with the AGC in Jun 2026 [S].
- **SEDA:**
  - Registered PV Investor (RPVI): paid-up capital RM1m local / RM10m foreign; foreign RPVIs only on
    projects >250 kWac and must use a registered service provider [P].
  - Registered PV Service Provider directory exists separately [S].
