Free desktop tool · Malaysia

Know your site's terrain before you commit.

Upload a KML or KMZ of your site and get a desktop terrain screening in minutes — elevation, slope classes under the KPKT hillside guidelines, cross-section profiles and the flags worth checking before you bid, buy or design.

What you get

  • Site layout map — your boundaries, routes and points on one sheet.
  • Elevation map with contours — where the high and low ground sits.
  • KPKT slope classification map — Class I to IV, with the share of each.
  • Terrain cross-sections — profiles across the site and along routes.
  • PDF and Word report — areas, slope statistics, flags and recommended follow-up studies.

What it is not

  • It is not a topographic or land survey, a geotechnical investigation or an engineering design.
  • It uses open ~30 m satellite elevation data, which includes tree canopy and buildings. Use it to decide what to study next — not to finalise a layout.
