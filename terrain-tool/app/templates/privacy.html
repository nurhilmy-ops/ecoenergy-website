3. Personal data we process

  • Your email address.
  • Optional answers on the upload page: site label, scheme, project stage and notes.
  • The site file you upload (KML/KMZ, including any names and descriptions inside it) and the results we derive from it: area, location (state, district and centre point), elevation and slope statistics, maps and your PDF/Word report. A site file is linked to your email address, and your notes or file may identify you or other people.
  • Your choices: whether EcoEnergy staff may email you about each site you submit, and whether you want our newsletter.
  • Consent records: what you agreed to or withdrew, when, the wording shown to you and the notice version, with the IP address and browser used at the time.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser user agent and timestamps; a session cookie while you are signed in and a short-lived security cookie when you confirm a link; one-way hashes of your email address and IP address used for abuse limits.
  • Source channel: the UTM tags in the link that brought you to the tool, and the domain of the website you came from (never the full page address).

Please do not include other people's personal data in your notes or files unless you are authorised to share it.

4. Where your data comes from

We collect it directly from you, automatically from your browser while you use the tool, and from the UTM tags in the link you followed.{% if turnstile_on %} Cloudflare Turnstile, our bot protection, tells us whether a request appears to come from a person.{% endif %} We do not obtain your personal data from any other source for this tool.

5. Why we process it

  1. To verify your email address and deliver your terrain screening report. This is required to use the tool.
  2. So that EcoEnergy staff can email you about a site you submitted, for example to clarify results or discuss next steps. We do this only if you tick the separate, optional box on the upload page for that site. The box is unticked by default and the choice is per report: you can change it at any time on the report page or on your account page.
  3. To send you our newsletter and insights by email, only if you tick the separate newsletter box and confirm your email address. This is voluntary.

We also use your data to keep the tool secure and prevent abuse, to see which posts or channels bring visitors to the tool, to keep records of your consent and to meet our legal obligations.

6. Is it obligatory?

Your email address is obligatory: without it we cannot verify you or send your report, so you cannot use the tool. A site file is needed to produce a report. Everything else is voluntary: the optional answers, staff follow-up and the newsletter. Leaving them blank or unticked does not affect your report. Technical data is collected automatically when the tool is used and is needed to run it securely.
