# 5. Detailed Bill of Quantities — BESS Plant (100 MW / 400 MWh reference)

Rates are **indicative Q3-2026 planning rates in RM** — for tender use, strip rates and float to market. Quantities assume a ~4.5-acre flat platform, firm ground (Option A footings); piling alternative shown in Bill 2A.
