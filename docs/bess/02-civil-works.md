# 2. Civil Works for the BESS Area

Structural and foundation design is governed by ground conditions established from the **JMG (Jabatan Mineral dan Geosains) regional geological data** and the project-specific **Soil Investigation (SI)**. No foundation type is fixed until SI results are in hand.

## 2.1 Site investigation scope (pre-design)

| Activity | Typical scope for a 2–5 acre BESS site |
|---|---|
| Desk study | JMG geological map & memoir, topo survey, flood records (JPS), previous land use |
| Boreholes | 4–8 nos. to 20–30 m or SPT N=50 refusal ×3, with SPT every 1.5 m |
| Mackintosh / JKR probes | 10–20 nos. across yard for shallow variability |
| Sampling & lab | UD samples: Atterberg, triaxial UU/CU, consolidation (soft clay), chemical (sulphate/chloride/pH for concrete durability), organic content (peat) |
| Field tests | Soil resistivity (Wenner, for earthing design), plate load test (raft option), infiltration test (drainage/OSD), groundwater standpipes |
| Topography | 0.5 m contour survey, platform level optimisation vs. flood level (typically platform ≥ 0.6 m above 100-yr flood level per JPS advice) |

## 2.2 Foundation selection matrix (driven by SI data)

| Ground condition (typical Malaysian setting) | Indicative SI signature | Foundation solution for BESS containers / PCS skids / trafo | Main trafo & e-house |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residual granite/sedimentary soils, firm to stiff (inland ex-estate land) | SPT N ≥ 10 within 3 m, low compressibility | RC **strip footings / pad-and-ground-beam plinths**, or 300 mm raft on 300 mm compacted crusher run | Pads/rafts, nominal |
| Loose sandy / fill platforms | N 4–10, granular | Compaction + **rigid raft** (350–400 mm) or short driven RC piles under heavy skids | Piled pads |
| Coastal alluvium / marine clay (Klang, Kuantan, coastal Sabah) | N < 4 upper 5–15 m, high PI, consolidation settlement | **Driven piles**: 150–200 mm spun micropiles or 200×200–250×250 RC square piles to set, with pile caps + ground beams under every container rail; or ground treatment (surcharge + PVD / stone columns) + raft if programme allows | Driven spun piles 300–400 mm |
| Peat / organic (parts of Sarawak, Johor) | Organic >20 %, very high settlement | Excavate & replace (shallow) or piles to competent stratum; avoid floating solutions for BESS mass (~35–45 t/container) | Piles mandatory |
| Ex-mining land (Kinta, Kuala Langat) | Erratic profile, slimes pockets | Borehole grid tightened; dynamic compaction / piles per pocket mapping | Piles |

**Loads to design for:** BESS container ~35–45 t operational on two rails (line load ~50–80 kN/m); PCS+MV skid 15–25 t; main transformer 60–90 t plus jacking/plinth loads; crane outrigger loads during installation (often governs hardstanding design).

## 2.3 Civil works packages

1. **Site clearing & earthworks** — clearing/grubbing, strip topsoil, cut & fill to platform, compaction 95 % modified Proctor in 300 mm layers, turfed/erosion-protected slopes ≤1:2, silt traps during construction (DOE/LDP2M2 compliance).
2. **Ground improvement / piling** — per §2.2; PDA/static load tests (1 preliminary + working tests at 1 % of piles or min 2).
3. **Foundations & plinths** — container plinths (twin RC rails or pads at ISO corner castings ±5 mm level tolerance), PCS/skid plinths, **transformer plinths with 110 % oil-volume bund** and oil interceptor per DOE, e-house strip/raft, lightning mast & gantry footings.
4. **Cable infrastructure** — trenches (sand bed + tiles), precast concrete-lined main routes, HDPE/uPVC duct banks with concrete surround at road crossings, draw pits, cable cellar/trench in e-house.
5. **Buildings** — e-house/control building (RC frame or prefab steel, IP-rated, air-conditioned N+1, raised floor or cable trench), guard house, store.
6. **Roads & hardstanding** — access + internal ring road 6 m wide, JKR flexible pavement (or crusher-run crane hardstanding), turning radii for 40-ft trailers & 100–250 t mobile crane.
7. **Drainage** — perimeter and internal drains sized to MSMA 2nd Ed., OSD/detention if required by local authority, discharge approval from JPS; platform cross-falls 1:150–1:200 away from plinths.
8. **Fencing & security** — 2.4 m anti-climb fence + 3-strand barbed overhang; separate TNB-spec compound fence at interconnection facility; motorised gate.
9. **Earthing civil interface** — grid trenching co-ordinated before pavements; test pits/chambers.
10. **Fire protection civil** — hydrant main and pump house/water tank if mandated by Bomba (site-specific; containerised LFP with certified suppression often negotiated to hydrant coverage only), fire access route 6 m.

## 2.4 Statutory & approvals touching civil scope

- Land conversion/zoning; DO/planning (PBT), building plans for e-house (PBT + Bomba), earthworks permit (LDP2M2), EIA if triggered (site >50 ha or sensitive), JPS drainage discharge, DOE scheduled waste plan (batteries), CIDB registration & levy, JKKP/DOSH notifications, G7 contractor for main civil package (typical).
