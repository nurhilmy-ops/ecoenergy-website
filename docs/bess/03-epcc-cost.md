# 3. EPCC Cost — BESS Facility in Malaysia (Q3-2026 basis)

## 3.1 Market benchmarks

| Benchmark | Size | Value | Implied unit cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solarvest CEO estimate for MyBEST-class EPCC (2024–25) | 100 MW / 400 MWh | ~RM400 mil | **~RM1,000/kWh (~USD 210/kWh)** |
| BESS Lahad Datu, Sabah (SESB off-taker; incl. 132 kV interconnection & substation) | 100 MW / 400 MWh | RM645 mil | ~RM1,610/kWh all-in |
| Global 4-h utility LFP EPC range 2025–26 (BNEF/NREL trend, landed Asia) | — | USD 140–220/kWh | RM 620–980/kWh |

Battery pack prices have fallen sharply (LFP cell oversupply), but Malaysian EPCC totals carry import logistics, SST, grid-compliance engineering, and land/civil premiums for soft-ground sites.

Drivers pushing toward **High**: piled foundations on marine clay/peat, 132 kV GIS interconnection, long cable/OHL route, grid-forming capability, N-1 main transformers, augmentation contracts, Sabah logistics (SESB projects typically +10–20 % on construction rates).

Drivers toward **Low**: firm ground (pad footings), existing PMU busbar spare bay at ≤33 kV, large single-order battery procurement timed to soft cell market, brownfield industrial land with utilities.

## 3.3 Costing assumptions & exclusions

- FX basis USD/MYR 4.2–4.5; battery & PCS are USD/CNY-denominated (≈65–75 % of EPCC is imported content) — carry FX and battery-index escalation clauses in the EPCC contract.
- SST on imported equipment and services per prevailing rules (batteries/PCS import duty typically 0 % under ATIGA/ACFTA from China; verify HS codes 8507.60 / 8504.40 at bid time).
- Excluded: land acquisition/premium, financing costs, TNB connection charges & deep reinforcement, insurance beyond CAR/EAR during construction, long-term service agreement (LTSA ~1.5–2.5 % of plant cost p.a.), augmentation capex (years 5–10, typically 10–20 % of initial battery cost), O&M.
- Programme: 14–20 months EPCC for 100 MW class (long-lead: main transformer 12–18 mths, 132 kV GIS 12–15 mths, batteries 6–9 mths).
