Privacy notice.

EcoEnergy Consultancy Sdn. Bhd.Effective 1 November 2026

This notice explains how EcoEnergy Consultancy Sdn. Bhd. ("EcoEnergy", "we") handles personal data you give us through this website, by email or by phone, as required by the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

What we collect

  • Your name, work email, phone number, company and role, when you give them to us.
  • Site and project details you choose to send (location, acreage, KML files, land title details).
  • The content of your messages to us.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If we add website analytics, this notice will be updated first.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange the consult you asked for.
  • To prepare a proposal or carry out an engagement you request.
  • To keep records required by law (tax, accounting, contracts).

We do not sell your data or add you to a mailing list without your consent.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run this website and our email: our form-handling service (FormSubmit), our email providers, and our website host (GitHub Pages). Some of them store data outside Malaysia; we use them only where the transfer is permitted under the PDPA. We share data with other parties only with your consent or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become engagements are deleted within 24 months of our last contact. Engagement records are kept for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, withdraw your consent, or ask us to stop processing it, subject to the PDPA. Write to contact@ecoconsultancy.services. We will reply within 21 days.

Is it compulsory?

Giving us your name and email is needed for us to reply. Everything else is optional.
